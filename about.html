Professional profile

Teaching, workforce pathways, and faculty development

Kristine has more than 25 years of experience building education-to-workforce pathways and developing technical curriculum. At Moraine Valley, she teaches across Computer Information Systems and Automation & Engineering Technology, including programming, web development, networking, robotics, Internet of Things, electronics, and mechatronics.

Her current work also includes national cybersecurity and AI initiatives, grant-funded program leadership, faculty capacity building, and the design of educational experiences that help students and educators engage with emerging technologies in practical ways.
